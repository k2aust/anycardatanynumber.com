Named number

Top or Face, the card's position, then q = position − number: plus = that many quads, minus = split that many blanks. Dashed border = uses the card's other copy, not the taught rule. Dark = not reachable. Tap a card for the instruction.

How to calculate

Jack = 1 · Queen = 5 · King = 6 — number cards are themselves. ♦♣  =  value + 20 ♥♠  =  value + 10  (number 20 or under) ♥♠  =  value + 30  (number 21 or over) q  =  card position  −  named number positive → that many quads, rest single pairs · negative → all single, then split that many blanks · zero → straight through

Level 1 — you name the suit: add the tens. Teens +10, twenties +20, thirties +30. +10 and +30 are hearts / spades, +20 is diamonds / clubs. Any colour and any value 11–40, never more than 10 quads.
Level 2 — they name the suit: the rule above. The whole card is free 16–30.

The deck

Top first. Every printed pair is a red card over a black card, faces inward. 48 pairs, 96 thin, 60 printed faces; only pairs 1–9 and 40–48 are ever split. Black reads J 2 3 4 Q K 7 8 9 10 straight down (spades, clubs, spades); red is the same run reversed on top (hearts, diamonds, hearts). A black card's number is its pair + 1; a red card's is 50 − its pair.

Level 3 — displacement deal

Pairs are roughed in twos. A quad turnover, then dealing one stuck pair, exposes the first pair's upper card — so an upper card can be reached from the far end when its pair is the first of its roughed group. On this deck that is every card whose number is odd (slots J, 3, Q, 7, 9):

Any card with an odd number can also be produced from the other end, at 52 − its number.

Red cards displace from the top, black from the face. It mostly widens the picture group: free suit 14–30 becomes 14–35. Turn on the toggle in the calculator to include these (marked †).

Every card's range

Spades equal hearts and clubs equal diamonds. † is the displacement deal. A position reaches down to position − half the pairs above it (quads) and up to position + 9 (blank splits), capped at 46.
